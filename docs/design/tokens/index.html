Surface / card specimen

Keep the content grounded.

Background, padding, corners and optional lift. No click behavior is implied.

Transparent group · painted silhouette

Filter, not box-shadow

filter: drop-shadow(…) follows painted content. An opaque card looks like a lifted box; a transparent group lifts its painted children. Nonzero filters create a stacking context. Compose other filters deliberately.

Shadow ink references palette.dark.4: 20% alpha at level 1, approximately 24% at levels 2–5. These are the original Adaptive recipes.
